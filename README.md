# Laddu Learning World

Laddu is an interest-led, play-based foundational learning web app for children. It combines the child's interests—comedy/funny/prank, family/vlog-style play, kids/cartoon worlds, story/horror/mystery/fantasy, DIY/craft/drawing, and music/dance—with foundational literacy, numeracy, creative expression, movement, social-emotional learning, problem solving and future skills.

## Current experience
- **English ABC:** A–Z recognition, sound-symbol connection, vocabulary and oral language.
- **Hindi Akshar:** swar/vyanjan recognition, sound-word association and oral language.
- **Numeracy:** number recognition and counting **1–100**, quantity, sequence, before/after, comparison and everyday math.
- **Mystery/fantasy stories:** prediction, clues, sequencing, safe choices and problem solving.
- **DIY & drawing:** fine-motor practice, free drawing, tracing, planning, pattern-making and explanation.
- **Music, rhymes & dance:** beat, listening, memory, movement and self-expression.
- **Comedy & family play:** respectful humour, empathy, communication, responsibility and initiative.
- **Additional activities:** memory, sorting, sequencing, nature, feelings/friendship, movement, mini-market and sound discrimination.

## NCF-FS-informed foundation-stage coverage
The design is informed by the National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage (NCF-FS) developmental domains: Physical Development; Socio-emotional and Ethical Development; Cognitive Development; Language and Literacy Development; Aesthetic and Cultural Development; and Positive Learning Habits. Activities emphasise play, discovery, doing, oral language, emergent literacy/numeracy, creativity, movement, real-life contexts and child choice.

## 12 future-facing skills
Critical thinking, creativity, communication, collaboration, problem solving, digital literacy, information literacy, adaptability, initiative, leadership, social-emotional learning, and productivity/self-management.

## Design principles
1. Interest-led: the child's six interests are used as the engagement layer rather than treated as separate entertainment.
2. Integrated: one activity can intentionally practise language, numeracy, social-emotional, physical, creative and thinking skills together.
3. Play before drill: repetition is embedded in stories, choices, movement, making and discovery.
4. Child agency: open-play and creative activities allow multiple valid paths.
5. Safe participation: comedy/pranks are framed as respectful and non-harmful; mystery play includes trusted-adult and safety reasoning.
6. Low-friction: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build system, local progress storage, speech synthesis and Web Audio where supported.

This is an educational prototype informed by the NCF-FS/NEP 2020 direction; it is **not an official NCF/NEP certification** and does not replace teacher/parent judgment. Progress is stored locally in the browser.
